# CareerBridge

### University Career & Internship Management Platform

CareerBridge is a web-based platform designed to connect university students, employers, career officers, and administrators through a centralized career and internship management system.

## Project Overview

The platform will simplify the university career and internship process by providing a centralized system for:

- Discovering internship and job opportunities
- Managing student applications
- Posting and managing opportunities
- Screening and shortlisting candidates
- Managing interviews and recruitment
- Monitoring internship placements
- Managing feedback and evaluations

## User Roles

- **Student**
- **Employer**
- **Career Officer**
- **Administrator**
